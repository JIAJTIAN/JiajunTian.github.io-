Professional Summary

University of Chicago Postdoctoral Scholar at NSF’s ChemMatCARS with experience in APS beamline commissioning, anomalous X-ray scattering, in situ and operando sample environments, controls integration, and automated data processing for dynamic soft and nanomaterials.

Experience

  • 2024 - present

    Lemont, Illinois

    Postdoctoral Scholar
    University of Chicago / NSF's ChemMatCARS, APS Sector 15
    Develop and commission beamline-integrated methods for anomalous X-ray scattering, multimodal in situ measurements, and dynamic nanomaterials characterization.
    • Develop an integrated continuous-flow microfluidic ASAXS/WAXS platform combining flow control, optical imaging, UV-Vis spectroscopy, X-ray detectors, and automated processing.
    • Contribute to ASAXS/AWAXS commissioning through detector and flight-tube installation, alignment, detector-geometry validation, and energy-dependent measurement workflows.
    • Commissioned an in situ UV-Vis spectroscopy capability and integrated it with X-ray scattering, optical imaging, and controlled flow.
    • Support user experiments from planning and sample-environment installation through alignment, acquisition, troubleshooting, reduction, and interpretation.
    • Develop Bluesky plans, a Queue Server client, HDF5 acquisition workflows, and FrameByFrame-ASWAXS reduction and analysis software.
    • Integrate and troubleshoot EPICS motors, area detectors, cameras, stages, triggering, metadata capture, and syringe- and pressure-driven flow controls.
    • Develop element-specific models for diselenide-containing liquid-crystal elastomers, contributing to a 2026 Chemistry of Materials publication.
  • 2019 - 2024

    Stony Brook, New York

    Graduate Research Assistant / Ph.D. Researcher
    Stony Brook University
    Investigated nanocellulose assembly, dynamics, flow response, and functional composites using synchrotron and laboratory characterization.
    • Designed and interpreted XPCS experiments at NSLS-II CHX and APS 8-ID to quantify cellulose-nanocrystal self-assembly, aging, arrested-state behavior, and flow/rest dynamics.
    • Investigated metal-ion-cross-linked nanocellulose antifreezing behavior and nanoparticle formation in nanocellulose scaffolds using in situ SAXS/WAXS and complementary characterization.
    • Developed nanocellulose-based environmental-remediation composites, including ZnO-nanocellulose and nitro-oxidized CNF/carbon-nitride systems.
    • Operated TEM and SEM/EDS at Brookhaven’s Center for Functional Nanomaterials and used cryo-TEM, DLS, zeta-potential measurements, and rheology.
  • 2016 - 2019

    Beijing, China

    Master's Researcher
    Beijing University of Chemical Technology
    Developed cyanostilbene-based photoresponsive molecules and cholesteric liquid-crystal systems.
    • Designed a sunlight-driven liquid-crystal platform for real-time in situ ultraviolet monitoring.
