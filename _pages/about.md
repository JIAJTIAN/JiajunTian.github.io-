<p class="cyber-hero-copy">I study how soft materials and nanocomposites form and evolve during reactions and processing. At NSF's ChemMatCARS, APS Sector 15, I develop in situ X-ray scattering experiments to follow these changes, with particular interests in flow-based measurements and nanocellulose-based materials for environmental applications.</p>

## CURRENT WORK

- **Following materials in flow.** I am developing a continuous-flow platform that combines X-ray scattering, UV-Vis spectroscopy, and optical imaging to connect structural changes with reaction and processing conditions.
- **Separating structural contributions.** I help commission ASAXS/AWAXS capabilities and develop energy-dependent measurements and analysis for multicomponent materials.

Alongside these projects, I support beamline users and develop [scientific software]({{ '/software/' | relative_url }}) for acquisition, reduction, and interpretation.
